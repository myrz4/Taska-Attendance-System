## Structure Rule Used In All Forms

- Workflow checks come first: `Pass / Fail / Not Tested`
- Experience ratings come next: `1-5`
- Final closed acceptance questions come after that
- Open-ended feedback is last

## Facilitator Notes

- Give each tester only the form for their role.
- Ask the tester to complete real role-based tasks before answering.
- Capture screenshots or short videos for every `Fail` answer.
- Record whether each issue is a usability problem, data problem, permission problem, or performance problem.
- Keep comments about the current demo payment flow separate from comments about a future real payment gateway.
